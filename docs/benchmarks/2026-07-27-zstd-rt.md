- **Machine:** Apple M3 Max, macOS, Metal build (`make macos`)
- **zstd library:** `zstd --version` → `*** Zstandard CLI (64-bit) v1.5.7, by Yann Collet ***`
- **Table:** `tables/ntlm_ascii-32-95#8-8_0_422000x1000000_0.rt`, 16,000,000 bytes (already sorted; verified with `crackalack_sort`)
- **Date:** 2026-07-27

> The benchmark table is 16 MB and fits entirely in page cache, so the raw `.rt`
> baseline is close to a memcpy and the compressed formats look worse on
> end-to-end lookup time than they would at 16 GiB. Ratio and throughput are
> size-independent and transfer; the lookup timings are directional only.

Peak RSS for `-d` (decompress) ranges ~31-33 MB across all levels — roughly
2x the raw table size, consistent with holding both the compressed input and
decompressed output resident at once. Task 7 aims to reduce this.

## Task 7: streaming decompression — before/after

`zst_decompress()` was rewritten to feed the input to `ZSTD_decompressStream`
in `ZSTD_DStreamInSize()`-sized chunks instead of reading the whole
compressed file into memory before calling `ZSTD_decompress()`. Full
level-sweep numbers for the streaming version are in
`2026-07-27-zstd-rt-streaming.md`/`.json`. Decompress throughput is within
noise of the one-shot version at every level (all within ~10%, several
faster). Peak RSS (`decompress_rss`, from `crackalack_rt2zst -d` under
`/usr/bin/time -l`):

| level | one-shot RSS | streaming RSS | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 33,079,296 | 23,576,576 | -9,502,720 (-28.7%) |
| 3 | 33,079,296 | 25,149,440 | -7,929,856 (-24.0%) |
| 9 | 32,407,552 | 27,213,824 | -5,193,728 (-16.0%) |
| 12 | 32,423,936 | 27,197,440 | -5,226,496 (-16.1%) |
| 19 (default) | 31,260,672 | 31,342,592 | +81,920 (+0.3%, noise) |
| 22 | 31,260,672 | 38,764,544 | +7,503,872 (+24.0%) |
| 19, 16 threads | 31,260,672 | 31,342,592 | +81,920 (+0.3%, noise) |

**Caveat:** at low compression levels the streaming rewrite delivers the
expected win — peak RSS drops by roughly the no-longer-buffered compressed
size. At level 19 (the project's default) and level 22, the improvement
disappears or reverses. This table (16 MB) is small enough that
`ZSTD_getFrameContentSize` reports a content size within the frame's window,
and at higher levels zstd selects a larger encoded window log to capture
long-distance matches; `ZSTD_decompressStream`'s internal `DCtx` allocates a
history/window buffer sized to that window log independently of our
single-shot output buffer, which offsets the savings from no longer holding
the whole compressed file in memory. The fix still removes the
compressed-file buffer unconditionally (verified via the level-1/3/9/12
deltas above), and it is not a regression at level 19 — RSS is flat within
measurement noise — but operators should not expect the ~8.7 MB
(`comp_bytes` at level 19) reduction the task's stated premise implied for
the table sizes actually exercised here. This should be called out in
review; it does not block the change since level 19 shows no measured
regression, only a smaller-than-hoped benefit.
